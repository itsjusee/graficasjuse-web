Art Direction, Visual Identities & Club Culture

Designing Valencia's Nightlife.

Over the past few years, I've worked with some of Valencia's most recognizable nightlife brands, shaping visual identities, developing campaigns and creating content that connects music, fashion and contemporary culture.

From building emerging club identities to reinterpreting the legacy of iconic venues, my work explores how design can shape nightlife experiences, build communities and give each space its own personality.

Roto Club · Spook · Oven Club · TOTAL

Roto Club

Building a club's personality.

I joined Roto when the brand had around 4K Instagram followers and helped shape its growth to 18K, developing a distinctive visual identity and communication style.

Inspired by street culture, fashion and the people on the dancefloor, I built a bold, unapologetic visual universe that placed the club's own community at the centre of its campaigns.

Working closely with photographer Manel, we developed seasonal campaigns featuring real clubgoers, creating a recognizable aesthetic that helped establish Roto's personality.

Beyond campaigns, I designed weekly flyers, digital content and merchandise, including a custom scarf that extended the brand beyond the club itself.

Spook

Reinterpreting a legacy.

Spook is an iconic institution in Valencia's electronic music history, deeply connected to the legendary Ruta del Bakalao.

Together with my colleague Erika, I work on expanding Spook's existing visual language while respecting the club's identity and cultural heritage.

By revisiting Spook's historical poster archive, I've explored ways to reinterpret its original graphic codes for contemporary events, connecting the club's past with its present. Keeping the legacy alive through contemporary design.

Oven Club

For Oven, one of Valencia's established house music venues, my work has covered different aspects of the brand.

I developed the visual identity and communication for its 13th anniversary, including campaign assets and merchandise that celebrated the club's history.

I also led the rebranding of Oven Bar, the venue's more cultural and social space, bringing together cocktails, listening sessions and live performances.

Inspired by house culture and everyday bar materials, the identity uses a minimal, flexible graphic system designed to adapt across programming, printed materials and digital communication.

Total

Queer as fuck.

TOTAL is one of Valencia's most exciting emerging queer parties, bringing together electronic music, self-expression and a growing community.

I work across its creative direction, graphic design and communication, developing a bold visual language inspired by queer internet culture.

From posters and merchandise to video campaigns, TikTok content and community-led activations, the brand embraces an irreverent, playful and provocative attitude.

Beyond aesthetics, the communication aims to strengthen a sense of belonging and celebrate the dancefloor as a space for freedom, expression and collective identity.

Reflections

Working in nightlife has given me the freedom to explore countless creative directions, experiment with different visual languages and constantly push my own boundaries.

It's taught me to think fast, work under pressure and find creative solutions within tight deadlines, while staying connected to emerging trends, internet culture and contemporary aesthetics.

I've also learned to build flexible, easy-to-use visual systems that other designers can adopt and evolve, ensuring brand consistency beyond my own involvement.
